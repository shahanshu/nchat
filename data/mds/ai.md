# SUBJECT: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE [Total: 80 Marks]
## Year: III | Part: II

**Unit 1: Introduction [4 Hours] [7 Marks]**
- 1.1. Definition and Importance of AI, AI and related fields, History of AI
- 1.5. Applications of AI
- 1.6. Definition and importance of Knowledge and Learning

**Unit 2: Problem Solving [4 Hours] [7 Marks]**
- 2.1. Defining problems as state space search, Problem formulation
- 2.3. Problem types, Well-defined problems, Constraint satisfaction problems
- 2.4. Game playing, Production systems

**Unit 3: Search Techniques [5 Hours] [9 Marks]**
- 3.1. Uninformed search: DFS, BFS, Depth limit search
- 3.2. Informed search: Hill climbing, Best first search, Greedy search, A* search
- 3.3. Adversarial search: Minimax procedure, Alpha-beta pruning

**Unit 4: Knowledge Representation, Inference and Reasoning [8 Hours] [14 Marks]**
- 4.1. Formal logic: Connectives, Truth tables, Syntax, Semantics, Tautology, Validity
- 4.2. Propositional and Predicate logic, FOPL, Horn clauses
- 4.3. Rules of inference, Unification, Resolution Refutation System (RRS)
- 4.4. Statistical Reasoning: Probability, Bayes' theorem, Causal networks, Belief networks

**Unit 5: Structured Knowledge Representation [4 Hours] [7 Marks]**
- 5.1. Representations and Mappings, Issues in Knowledge Representation
- 5.4. Semantic nets, Frames, Conceptual dependencies, and Scripts

**Unit 6: Machine Learning [6 Hours] [10 Marks]**
- 6.1. Concepts of learning, Learning by analogy, Inductive learning
- 6.2. Explanation-based learning, Neural networks, Genetic algorithms
- 6.5. Fuzzy learning, Boltzmann Machines

**Unit 7: Applications of AI [14 Hours] [26 Marks]**
- 7.1. Neural networks: Adaline, Perceptron, Multilayer Perceptron, Back Propagation, Hopfield, Kohonen
- 7.2. Expert Systems: Architecture, Knowledge acquisition, Declarative/Procedural knowledge
- 7.3. NLP and Machine Vision: Phonetic, Syntactic, Semantic, Pragmatic levels
